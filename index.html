Brew the cup you liked, again.

Pourmemo is an iPhone app for keeping your favorite pour-over recipes and following them step by step when you brew. Your recipe library is stored on your iPhone, with manual backup export when you choose it.

Example: Weekday V60

15 g coffee · 250 g water

  1. 0:00  Bloom, pour to 45 g
  2. 0:40  Pour to 150 g
  3. 1:15  Pour to 250 g
  4. Swirl and let it drain

What Pourmemo does

Save a recipe

A recipe needs two numbers: the coffee dose and the total water. Everything else is optional. Add pour steps written as running totals ("pour to 150 g"), so you always know where the scale should read. A step can also be just an action, such as "swirl". You can add a reference start time to any step if you like to brew by the clock.

Prepare and brew

Tap a recipe, or Brew on the home screen, to open Prepare. Before brewing, adjust the coffee dose for this cup. Total water and any filled-in cumulative water targets scale with it, rounded to whole grams. Temperature, grind and reference times stay as written. Your saved recipe is unchanged.

Tap Start brewing to see one step at a time, with its cumulative water target and a count-up timer. Move forward or back manually, pause or resume the timer, or open All steps & recipe for the full overview. Water amounts are targets, not live measurements. The screen stays awake while the brewing screen is active.

Decide what to save

After a brew with a changed dose, you can update the original recipe or save a new copy. Both options open the editor; nothing is saved until you tap Save. Or tap Done to finish without changes. You can also use Copy & adjust from a recipe's menu. A new copy counts toward the five free recipe slots; editing an existing recipe does not.

Keep your list tidy

Search your recipes, mark favorites, rename them, or delete the ones you no longer brew.

Choose Compare recipes from a recipe's menu to view two saved recipes together. This does not modify either recipe or keep a brew-history log.

Free and Pourmemo Monthly

The free version includes the whole brewing flow and lets you create up to 5 saved recipes. Existing recipes and full backup restore stay usable even if your library contains more than five.

Pourmemo Monthly costs US$4.99 per month (or the equivalent price shown in your App Store region) and removes the five-recipe limit for saving new or copied recipes while your subscription is active. There is no free trial.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically each month unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings at any time.

If you go over the free limit or your subscription ends, none of your recipes are deleted or locked. You can still open, brew, edit and export every one of them. You just can't add another new or copied recipe until you are back under 5 or subscribe again.

Restore a purchase

  1. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  2. Open Pourmemo and go to the subscription screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Restoring purchases checks for an active subscription associated with your Apple Account; it does not renew an expired subscription. Your recipes are restored separately from a backup file (see below).

Cancel your subscription

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Pourmemo Monthly and tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep your subscription until the end of the period you already paid for. Apple handles all billing and refunds; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Back up and move your recipes

Backup is free for everyone. Pourmemo can export your whole recipe library as a single .json file. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere else you choose. To bring the library back, for example on a new iPhone, import that file and Pourmemo replaces the current library with the whole library from that file after you confirm. It does not merge libraries. Unsaved drafts are not included in exported backups.

Pourmemo does not upload your recipes anywhere by itself, so a backup file is the way to keep a copy off your phone. Deleting the app deletes the recipes stored on that iPhone.

If the recipe library on your iPhone is ever damaged and can't be opened, Pourmemo lets you export the original data as Pourmemo-original-recovery.bin so it isn't lost. That recovery file can't be imported back; only .json backups can. If this happens, email us and keep the file.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Does Pourmemo work offline?

You can open, edit and brew saved recipes offline, and create recipes within the free limit. Creating beyond five requires a subscription with a verified, unexpired entitlement; if its known expiry has passed, reconnect to refresh it. Buying or restoring a subscription needs the App Store to be reachable.

Will the timer move to the next step for me?

No. The timer only counts up. Use Next step and Previous step to move manually. All steps & recipe opens the full overview when you need it.

Why are my steps written as "pour to" amounts?

Most brewers watch the scale's running total while pouring. Writing each step as the total to reach means you never have to add numbers in your head mid-pour.

I have more than 5 recipes and no subscription. What happens?

Nothing is removed. You can view, brew, edit and export all of them. Only adding new or copied recipes is paused.

Which devices does it run on?

Pourmemo is made for iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy covers the Pourmemo: Pour Over Recipes app for iPhone ("Pourmemo"). The app stores your recipes on your iPhone and does not automatically send them to us. Apple purchases, files you choose to export, support emails and visits to this website are described below.

What we collect

Pourmemo has no developer account or sign-up, and the app does not automatically send your recipes or personal information to us. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code, and we do not run a server for the app.

Your recipes

The recipes you create (names, coffee and water amounts, pour steps, times and notes) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We cannot see them.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the app's data, as with any app. Those backups are handled by Apple under your device settings and Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Backup files you export

When you choose to export a backup, Pourmemo creates a .json file containing your recipes and hands it to the location you pick, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Where that file goes and who can see it is up to you. When you import a backup, Pourmemo reads only the file you select.

If your recipe library on the phone ever becomes damaged and can't be opened, Pourmemo offers to export the original library data as a recovery file (Pourmemo-original-recovery.bin) so nothing is thrown away. Like a backup, this only happens when you choose it, and the file goes wherever you save it. It is not sent to us. This file can't be imported back into the app; it is kept only so the original data is not lost.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple's StoreKit on your device whether your subscription is active; Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Pourmemo does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. It keeps the screen awake only while follow mode is open, and that setting stays on your device.

Emails you send us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and do not share it or add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information when you visit it; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

Pourmemo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.
